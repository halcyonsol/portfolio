ABOUT ME

Winston Kim.

MUSIC TECH / ART 101

My name is Winston Kim and I am a Music Tech major. My creative interests lean towards nostalgic/surreal, and slightly dystopian. I like experimenting with ideas that feel familiar in the beginning but become more strange the longer you interact with them.

I'm interested in how technology influences our perception of reality, time, and memory. I'm drawn to the contrast between old and new, where the imperfections of analog coincide with the idea of speculative future that has a feeling of nostalgia. I don't necessarily want everything I create to have some big meaning but sometimes just want to create things that are weird and see where it goes.
